Operations & GIS Portal (OM-Map) — Privacy Policy
Effective Date: September 22, 2026

1. Overview & Institutional Commitment

The National Irrigation Administration (NIA) — Regional Office IV-B (MIMAROPA) is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, integrity, and security of personnel and institutional data.

This Privacy Policy outlines how the NIA Region IV-B Operations & GIS Portal (OM-Map) collects, processes, and protects your information when authenticating via third-party identity providers, specifically Meta (Facebook / Messenger) and Google Sign-In, and when utilizing the portal's single integrated application: Maintenance and Status of Irrigation Facilities.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to authenticate personnel and manage role-based civil infrastructure workflows:

  • Meta (Facebook / Messenger) Profile Data: When you choose to sign in via Meta, we request access to your public_profile and email. This provides your full name, verified email address (if associated), public profile picture (used exclusively as your in-portal avatar), and unique Facebook User ID (UID).
  • Google OAuth Profile Data: When authenticating via Google, we receive your verified Google account name, email address, avatar photo, and Google unique ID.
  • Official Personnel Registration Profile: Submitted voluntarily by applicants during initial account setup:
    • Official NIA Designation (e.g., Senior Engineer, Division Manager, Field Inspector)
    • Designated NIA Office (Regional Office IV-B, MOMARO IMO, Occidental Mindoro IMO, or Palawan IMO)
    • Official Mobile Contact Number (for two-factor administrative verification and inspection alerts)
    • Assigned National Irrigation System (NIS) Scope

3. Purpose of Processing & Use of Data

Collected information is used exclusively for legitimate operational and administrative purposes:

  • Identity Verification: Ensuring that only authorized government personnel and accredited stakeholders access regional irrigation GIS networks and maintenance databases.
  • 6-Tier Institutional Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Enforcing jurisdictional access tiers (Master Developer, RO Administrator, IMO Administrator, Evaluator, Reviewer, Field Personnel, and Viewer).
  • Field Inspection Attribution: Crediting submitted Form 691 maintenance reports, photos, and canal status evaluations to the verified inspector.
  • Administrative Review: Allowing designated Regional and IMO gatekeepers to review and approve access requests within their statutory office boundary.

4. Data Storage, Protection & Security

All user profile records, authentication identifiers, and role assignments are stored securely on Google Cloud Firestore using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.

We do not store plain-text passwords. Session tokens are managed securely in strict compliance with OAuth 2.0 protocols. Access to administrative queues is protected by multi-tier cryptographic and server-side jurisdictional checks.

5. Non-Disclosure & Commercial Non-Sale Guarantee

We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data. Your information is never disclosed to commercial advertisers, marketing brokers, or external third-party platforms. Data is shared exclusively within authorized NIA operational divisions for official civil irrigation reporting.

6. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You retain the right at any time to inspect your profile details, update your contact numbers, or request complete deletion of your registration and OAuth associations from our database.

For step-by-step instructions on how to unlink your Facebook account or submit an automated/manual data purge request, please visit our official User Data Deletion Instructions Page.
